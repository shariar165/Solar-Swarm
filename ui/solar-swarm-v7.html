Shock geometry — 4-spacecraft timing model
The classic Cluster/MMS method: contact times at four non-coplanar craft give the shock normal n and speed V from (ri−r0)·n/V = ti−t0. A single L1 monitor cannot do this. Timing error ±2 s is assumed (1-s magnetometer).
Earth's magnetosphere response model
Compression from Rankine–Hugoniot (γ = 5/3, fast-magnetosonic Mach number), magnetopause from Shue et al. (1998), bow shock from Farris & Russell (1994). Ambient wind n = 5 cm⁻³, B = 5 nT, T = 10⁵ K and the sheath Bz are assumed.

What motion can and cannot do
Can: tune the S2–S5 baseline (a few 1,000 km) to the size of the structures — the same idea as MMS and Cluster changing their tetrahedron size ("focusing a telescope"), but decided automatically from forecast uncertainty.

Cannot: move the 100,000s-km tetrahedron (weeks of orbit design), or move anything meaningfully in the minutes between S1 contact and L1 contact.
